Can't sleep? Count sheep online

This free counting sheep website shows one calm sheep at a time hopping over a fence, every three seconds. Follow the rhythm, keep your breathing slow, and let your mind settle. No ads, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Does counting sheep actually work?

It is a simple, monotonous task that gives a busy mind something gentle to focus on instead of worries. Some research suggests that imagining a calm scene helps certain people fall asleep faster than counting alone, so picture the meadow too. This is not a medical treatment: if sleeplessness lasts for weeks, talk to a doctor.

How to use this sleep aid

Lie down, lower your screen brightness, switch on the soft noise if you like, and choose a sleep timer. The screen slowly fades to dark and the sheep stop on their own.
